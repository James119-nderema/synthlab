Privacy attributes

Quasi-identifiers can identify a student when combined. Sensitive attributes are outcomes or details the release should protect.

Public numeric bounds

Numeric values are clipped to these bounds and binned before synthesis. Use valid public limits (for example, official score ranges), not ranges inferred from private student records.

Dataset risk scan

The scan checks whether selected quasi-identifier combinations form groups smaller than five. It is a screening diagnostic, not a differential privacy guarantee.
